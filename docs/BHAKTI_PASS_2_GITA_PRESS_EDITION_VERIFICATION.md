1. **VR1 is an English counterpart to the approved VR2** — rejected by the title pages. VR1 is Sanskrit/Hindi; VR2 is Sanskrit/English. English VR1 quotations remain unapproved.

7. **A web transcription is itself Gita Press evidence** — rejected. Only the owner-specified originals and local RCM title-page copy support this ledger.
8. **“Service continues” is a verbatim scriptural line or proof of one single finale event** — not approved. It is an interpretation joining several separately located events.
