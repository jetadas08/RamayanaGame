# Follow Hanumān: Bhakti enrichment audit and content plan

**Status:** editorial plan, 23 September 2026. No player-facing content or gameplay is changed by this audit.

## A. Executive summary

**Readiness: 6/10.** The 35-encounter story, observation, mastery, memory, and object structure offers good places for small devotional aftertones. The limiting factor is source readiness: the runtime catalog marks Chapter I and Chapters VI–VIII citations `pendingEditionAudit`, and most Chapter II–V references `planningReference`. The older imported master workbook's `VERIFIED_*` flags are research leads, not evidence that the current Gita Press edition citations have passed audit. The approved commentary text and a specific Gita Press Rāmacaritamānasa edition are not present in this checkout.

Recommend **15 definite additions**, **8 optional additions** (only if they add something distinct after editorial review), and **12 encounters left unchanged**. The strongest opportunity is the ring's three-stage journey—entrusting, delivery, return token—without repeating one explanation three times. The greatest risk is saturation: if each close, mastery, object, memory, and chapter card restates “service,” the adventure becomes a commentary feed. The largest publication risk is treating provisional sarga/doha leads, or a web parallel text, as a verified Gita Press quotation. **No exact Sanskrit, Awadhi, transliteration, or translated verse is approved in this plan.** All draft lines below are editorial paraphrases or interpretive concepts, never source quotations.

The design test is: *observe → infer → experience → understand → remember*. A devotional line normally appears after story resolution or behind an opt-in layer. Nothing here changes completion, stars, source precedence, or progress.

### Reading the plan

- **Decision:** E = enrich; O = optional, gated by distinct value; N = no new enrichment.
- **Sources:** VR = Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa, Gita Press; RCM = Rāmacaritamānasa, Gita Press; HPS = Hari Prasad Shastri translation as a comparison aid; C = approved Sundara Kāṇḍa commentary, if its actual text is supplied and reviewed. Later tradition is never silently promoted into VR or RCM.
- **Readiness:** PEA = existing `pendingEditionAudit`; PR = existing `planningReference`; EVR = **edition verification required for the proposed new item**, even when the encounter itself has a provisional source range. None means `verified`.
- **Placement:** SC = story closing; GD = Go Deeper/★★★; ED = Explore Deeper; CM = character memory; SO = sacred object; CC = chapter completion; F = finale. **Level** 1 is one light line; 2 is short reflection plus source context; 3 is a deliberate tradition comparison. For each candidate below, the placement in the matrix is its *one primary home*, not permission to copy it to every surface.
- **Priority:** P0 = source check before any implementation; P1 = verify before publication; P2 = consider later. A candidate can have design priority P1 while its citation is blocked at P0.
