# Chapter VIII — Mission Fulfilled

Chapter VIII completes the playable Follow Hanumān campaign through four sequential encounters:

- HFF-01 — Rāvaṇa Falls
- HFF-02 — Hanumān Brings Sītā the News
- HFF-03 — Hanumān Goes Ahead to Bharata
- HFF-04 — Ayodhyā — Service Continues

The chapter keeps the Campaign Map → Chapter Map → Encounter → Explore → Story → Mastery → Complete architecture. Each encounter uses a bespoke observation or memory interaction before the canonical story reveal, three non-blocking mastery activities, a separate locked/revealed cinematic pair, and a meaningful three-star memory. HFF-03 resolves Hanumān → Bharata as Messenger inline. HFF-04 opens a dedicated campaign finale with the eight-chapter recap, relationship memories, both Sacred Object transmission trails, and replay destinations.

Progress normalization preserves existing saves and stores Chapter VIII completion separately in `finaleCompletedNodes`. `campaignComplete` is derived from HFF-04, while Complete and Mastered remain separate states. Full encounter IDs are used throughout reward, mastery, chapter, and replay logic to prevent similarly numbered encounters from sharing data.

The source layer retains Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa ranges with pending edition-audit notes rather than exposing editorial codes in gameplay. The pearl necklace and Bharata's sandals remain story artifacts; the V1 Sacred Object collection remains Rāma's Ring and Sītā's Cūḍāmaṇi.

The campaign catalog contains 35 planned records. Thirty-three encounters currently have approved playable content and therefore provide 99 mastery stars. The two Chapter II source-review records remain planned instead of being fabricated; totals are derived from playable campaign data.
