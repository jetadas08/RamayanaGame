## E. Mandodarī proof note

The original VR2 page images correct the prior page span. In **Sundara 10:50–53, printed pp.78–79**, Hanumān sees an adorned woman asleep on a splendid couch; the edition names **Mandodarī** and says he infers she is Sītā, rejoicing at first. Verse **10:54, p.79** continues his joyful reaction. **Sundara 11:1–4, all on p.79** then states that he dismisses that thought, considers Sītā's separation from Rāma and the incongruity of sleep, adornment, and drink, concludes the woman is someone else, and resumes the search. The app may portray observation → mistaken inference → correction. It must not present the palace woman as Sītā or add an unprinted private motive to Hanumān's reasoning. **No exact quote approved** for this scene.

## G. Setu proof note

The two approved excerpts support a **labeled comparison**, not a blended verse. VR2 Yuddha 22:48 has **Nala** offer to build the bridge; the surrounding passage describes construction by monkey workers. RCM Laṅkā doha 3 credits **Rāma's glory** for rocks floating; adjacent caupāīs mention Nala and Nīla. Neither inspected passage says Rāma's name was written on stones. That appealing motif remains **DO NOT USE as a Gita Press VR/RCM claim** until a separate approved witness is verified. Hanumān's earlier Sundara leap is a different event and route from the later Setu.
