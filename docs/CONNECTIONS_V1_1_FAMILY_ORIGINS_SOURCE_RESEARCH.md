# Connections V1.1 — Family & Origins source research

Research dossier only. This document does not authorize new graph nodes, edges, biographies, or exact quotations. It preserves the distinction between the reviewed feature slice and the next content layer.

## A. Research summary

The approved Gita Press *Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa* supports concise profiles of Añjanā, Keśarī, and Vāyu. Kiṣkindhā Kāṇḍa 4.66 names Añjanā (earlier Puñjikasthalā), identifies her as Keśarī's wife, and narrates Vāyu's role in Hanumān's birth. Sundara Kāṇḍa 5.35 and Uttara Kāṇḍa 7.35–36 add distinct retrospective accounts. The approved Gita Press *Rāmacaritamānasa* passages reviewed call Hanumān a son of the wind and emphasize his service to Rāma; they do not supply the same detailed family biography. These traditions must stay visibly separate.

**Evidence set.** All printed-page references below were checked against page images, not just OCR. The original Part 1 scan has no searchable text; its separate OCR copy was used only to locate pages. Part 2 and the Rāmacaritamānasa scan were also checked visually. No other online edition is treated as Gita Press evidence.

## B. Añjanā dossier

### Core identity and earlier identity

- **TEXT SAYS:** Jāmbavān names the apsaras Puñjikasthalā, later known as Añjanā; she became a vānarī through a curse and was Keśarī's wife. The account also calls her the daughter of the vānar leader Kuñjara. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.66.8–9, printed p. 922. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE. **APP MAY SAY:** “Añjanā, Hanumān's mother, is described in Jāmbavān's account as having earlier been the apsaras Puñjikasthalā.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** a specific curse-giver, offense, duration, or release condition. The cited verses give none.
- **TEXT SAYS:** She is Keśarī's wife; 4.66.10–20 narrates Vāyu's approach and her giving birth to the child. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.66.8, 10–20, printed pp. 922–923; compare V2, Uttara 7.35.19–21, p. 970. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE for the relationship and birth; DEFER for a detailed player-facing retelling of the encounter. **APP MAY SAY:** “The account names Keśarī as her husband and attributes Hanumān's birth to Añjanā and Vāyu.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** “Keśarī and Vāyu are interchangeable biological fathers,” or describe the encounter as an uncomplicated consensual romance. Añjanā objects in 4.66.14–16, which requires careful editorial treatment if the episode is narrated.
- **TEXT SAYS:** In the separate Uttara account, Keśarī rules on Sumeru, Añjanā is his wife, and she gives birth after Vāyu's involvement. After the gods grant boons, Vāyu returns the child to her. **Source:** V2, Uttara 7.35.19–21, p. 970; 7.36.27, p. 976. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE NOTE. **APP MAY SAY:** “A later retrospective passage in the same epic again names Añjanā as Keśarī's wife and Hanumān's mother.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that Uttara 7.35 independently states the Puñjikasthalā identity; it does not in the checked lines.

### Curse boundaries and meaning

The curse in Kiṣkindhā 4.66.9 concerns **Añjanā's transformation**. The sages' curse in Uttara 7.36.31–35 concerns **young Hanumān's awareness of his strength** after disruptive childhood acts. They are not one curse. The first account leaves the curse-giver, reason, and release terms unstated; no later/popular explanation has been verified to this project's source standard. A meaning candidate is that origins in the epic are told through remembered relationships, but that is a `learningInterpretation`, not a textual claim about why she was cursed.

## C. Keśarī dossier

- **TEXT SAYS:** Keśarī is a vānar and Añjanā's husband. The origin recital calls Hanumān his `kṣetraja` son while calling him Vāyu's `aurasa` son; these are different relational terms. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.66.8, 29–30, printed pp. 922, 924. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE; the Sanskrit terms themselves NEEDS QUOTE PROOF before exact on-screen quotation. **APP MAY SAY:** “Keśarī is Hanumān's father in the family lineage; the same passage separately names Vāyu's divine role in his birth.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** “Keśarī alone begot Hanumān,” or that the two fatherhood claims are identical.
- **TEXT SAYS:** Hanumān tells Sītā that Keśarī, his father, went from Mount Mālyavān to Gokarṇa and killed the demon Sambasādana in response to the sages. Hanumān then relates Vāyu's part in his birth. **Source:** V2, Sundara 5.35.80–83 and 90, printed pp. 164–165. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE NOTE. **APP MAY SAY:** “Hanumān himself remembers Keśarī as a capable vānar who aided sages.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that the textual Gokarṇa is a verified modern town or that Keśarī's exploit was Hanumān's own.
- **TEXT SAYS:** Keśarī appears among the vānar forces gathered for Sugrīva's campaign. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.39.18, printed p. 864. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE. **APP MAY SAY:** “Keśarī is named among the assembled vānar leaders.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that he is a principal speaking character in the encounter gameplay.
- **TEXT SAYS:** A retrospective Uttara passage locates Keśarī's rule on Sumeru. **Source:** V2, Uttara 7.35.19–21, printed p. 970. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE NOTE. **APP MAY SAY:** “The Uttara account associates Keśarī with Sumeru.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that Sumeru fixes a modern birthplace or route coordinate.

**Meaning boundary:** “Family and divine agency are both part of Hanumān's narrated origin” is a restrained `learningInterpretation`. Neither the cited passages nor this dossier establishes a detailed doctrine of fatherhood.

## D. Vāyu dossier

- **TEXT SAYS:** Vāyu is the wind deity in the origin account. He approaches Añjanā, promises a son endowed with speed and strength, and is identified in the account as Hanumān's begetter. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.66.10–20, 29–30, printed pp. 922–924. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE for the basic relation; DEFER for a simplified romantic birth scene. **APP MAY SAY:** “The Vālmīki account links Hanumān's birth and extraordinary speed with Vāyu, the wind deity.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that Keśarī is absent from the account or that Añjanā's objection did not occur.
- **TEXT SAYS:** Hanumān tells Sītā he was born through Vāyu's agency in Keśarī's household. **Source:** V2, Sundara 5.35.83, 90, printed pp. 164–165. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR PARAPHRASE. **APP MAY SAY:** “Hanumān himself speaks of both Keśarī and Vāyu when describing his origin.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that this passage cancels one relationship in favor of the other.
- **TEXT SAYS:** After the child is struck by Indra's thunderbolt, Vāyu withdraws the wind; the gods grant boons. A later account has Vāyu bring the child home to Añjanā. **Source:** V1, Kiṣkindhā 4.66.21–28, printed pp. 923–924; V2, Uttara 7.36.27, p. 976. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE NOTE. **APP MAY SAY:** “Vāyu acts as a protective father in the childhood accounts.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that the two separately located tellings are a single verbatim passage.
- **TEXT SAYS:** The approved Rāmacaritamānasa invokes Hanumān as `pavanakumāra` and Jāmbavān addresses him as `pavana tanaya` before the ocean crossing. **Source:** M1, Bāla Soraṭhā 17, p. 44; Kiṣkindhā chaupāī group, p. 761. **Classification:** `textualFact`. **Status:** READY FOR SOURCE NOTE; the exact Awadhi/Romanized wording NEEDS QUOTE PROOF. **APP MAY SAY:** “The Rāmacaritamānasa calls Hanumān a son of the wind and turns attention toward his service to Rāma.” **APP MUST NOT SAY:** that these lines explain Añjanā's curse or Keśarī's genealogy.

**Epithet discipline:** “son of the wind” is supported by the cited sources. Do not silently replace the printed Rāmacaritamānasa forms with the unattested-in-these-lines exact label “Pavanaputra,” or claim that every epithet appears in every work. A glossary may explain Vāyu/Marut/Pavana as wind-language only after each exact form is checked at its own locator.

## J. Source gaps

1. **Añjanā's earlier curse specifics — DEFER.** V1 4.66.9 states a curse but not its giver, cause, or release. Do not fill the gap from memory or popular retellings. The sages' later curse of Hanumān in V2 7.36 is a different event.
2. **Later and regional variants — DEFER.** No distinct edition/page has been verified here for named alternative birth narratives, modern birthplace claims, or expanded Keśarī genealogy. Those traditions require their own labeled research packet if desired.
3. **RCM family detail — DEFER as an RCM attribution.** The RCM passages checked establish wind-son devotional language, not the Kiṣkindhā 4.66 family recital. A complete negative claim about the entire RCM is not warranted by this targeted review.
4. **Exact Sanskrit/Awadhi terms — NEEDS QUOTE PROOF.** `kṣetraja`, `aurasa`, `pavanakumāra`, and `pavana tanaya` were identified at approved-edition page images, but no final line-by-line transcription/collation has been signed off for player-facing quotation.
5. **Birth encounter treatment — DEFER detailed copy.** V1 includes Añjanā's objection. Any extended narrative needs sensitive editorial review that accurately represents the passage without euphemism or invented consent.
6. **Geography — DEFER modern coordinates.** Mālyavān, Gokarṇa, and Sumeru are textual place names here; none has been established as a precise modern map point for this feature.
